I believe meaningful change begins when a community is invited to speak, listened to with patience, and trusted to help shape the solution. As Community Coordinator, my focus is to build that trust across every village and neighbourhood where Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha works.

Many families do not need a short-lived promise; they need dependable support that respects their experience and protects their dignity. I work with local volunteers, women’s groups, teachers, and health workers to understand the practical challenges people face every day. Together, we connect families with our education, health, sanitation, and livelihood initiatives in a way that is clear, welcoming, and accountable.

I am especially proud of the small moments that often go unnoticed: a parent returning to a learning session, a senior citizen receiving a check-up without fear, or a young volunteer discovering that their time can make someone’s life easier. These moments show us that service is not only about the size of an event. It is about consistency, respect, and showing up again and again.

My view is that every programme should leave behind stronger local relationships and greater confidence among the people we serve. If we continue working together with honesty and compassion, our community will not simply receive change; it will lead it.
