SANCHAITA MONDAL, Executive Member, Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha(DDSS)
Being associated with Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha as an Executive Member has been a meaningful and enriching experience for me. My journey with the organisation has given me an opportunity to work closely with people from different sections of society and to understand their needs, challenges and aspirations.
Through my involvement in the activities of the Sanstha, I have learned that social service is not only about providing support but also about creating awareness, encouraging participation and helping people become more confident and self-reliant. Working together with fellow members and volunteers has taught me the importance of teamwork, responsibility, compassion and commitment.
The various initiatives undertaken by Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha have allowed me to contribute, even in small ways, towards the welfare and development of our community. Meeting people, listening to their concerns and participating in activities for their benefit has been a deeply satisfying experience.
I feel proud to be a part of an organisation that is committed to social welfare and community development. My association with Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha has strengthened my belief that collective effort, empathy and sincere service can bring positive change to society.
I look forward to continuing my contribution to the Sanstha and working with all members towards building a more caring, inclusive and empowered community.
