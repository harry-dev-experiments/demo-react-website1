My association with Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha has been a deeply meaningful and enriching journey. As a social worker, I have had the opportunity to work closely with the organisation and participate in various initiatives aimed at improving the lives of people and strengthening the community.
My work with the Sanstha has allowed me to understand the challenges faced by people from different sections of society. Interacting directly with community members has taught me that social work requires not only dedication and hard work, but also empathy, patience and a genuine willingness to listen and understand people's needs.
One of the most rewarding aspects of my experience has been working together with the members, volunteers and other social workers of the organisation. Their commitment to community welfare has encouraged me to contribute more actively to social causes. I have learned that when people come together with a common purpose, even small efforts can create meaningful and lasting change.
My journey with Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha has strengthened my belief in the importance of service, equality and community participation. I feel privileged to contribute to the organisation's social welfare activities and to be part of its efforts towards creating a more caring, aware and empowered society.
I look forward to continuing my work with Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha and contributing my time, experience and efforts towards the welfare of people and the development of the community
