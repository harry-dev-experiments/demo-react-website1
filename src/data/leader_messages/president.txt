It is a matter of great pride and responsibility for me to serve as the President of Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha. Our organisation was formed with the belief that sincere service, collective effort and community participation can bring meaningful change to society.
Through the work of our Sanstha, we strive to understand the needs of people and respond to them with compassion and dedication. Our focus is not only on providing immediate support but also on creating awareness, encouraging self-reliance and helping people move towards a better and more dignified life.
Over the years, my association with the organisation has taught me that an NGO can make a real difference when its members, volunteers and community work together with honesty and commitment. Every activity, whether small or large, gives us an opportunity to reach people, understand their concerns and contribute towards their welfare.
I strongly believe that social development is a shared responsibility. Government institutions, voluntary organisations and ordinary citizens must work together to build an inclusive and progressive society. At Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha, we therefore encourage teamwork, transparency, compassion and active participation from the community.
I am grateful to all our members, volunteers, supporters and well-wishers for their valuable contributions. Their dedication gives strength to our mission and motivates us to continue our work with greater determination.
Our journey is ongoing, and there is still much to be done. I am confident that with unity, sincerity and continued community support, Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha will be able to reach more people and contribute meaningfully to the welfare and development of society.
