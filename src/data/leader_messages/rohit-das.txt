Young people are not only the future of our community; they are an important part of its strength today. As Youth Program Lead, I want every young person to have a safe space to learn, contribute, ask questions, and discover the confidence that comes from being useful to others.

Our youth initiatives combine practical learning with service. Through workshops, sports activities, environmental drives, digital learning sessions, and volunteer opportunities, we encourage young people to develop skills while staying connected to the needs around them. A certificate may recognise participation, but the real achievement is when a young volunteer learns to organise a team, support a child, or speak up for a healthier neighbourhood.

I have seen how quickly young people respond when they are trusted with responsibility. They bring energy, creativity, and an understanding of new ideas that can improve the way our programmes reach families. My responsibility is to guide that energy with care, make participation inclusive, and ensure that every voice receives an opportunity to be heard.

I hope our work creates a generation that measures success not only by personal progress, but also by the number of people they help carry forward. When young people choose empathy and action, the entire community moves closer to a more hopeful and sustainable future.
