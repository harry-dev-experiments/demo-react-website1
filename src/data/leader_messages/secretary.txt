Since 2007 of its registration with the Govt. of West Bengal under the Society Registration Act, 1860, the Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha(DDSS) has been serving to the society targeting to the marginal and under-privileged people for the work of mainstreaming them specially in the field of Health, Education and some basic needs.  Our organization has set some goals for immediate relief of the endangered people in the society suffering from different issues including basic needs of survival. Accordingly, for the last 25 years, the DDSS has been doing its social work incessantly.  Its self-motivated integrity in absolute social work has gained lots of benevolence and soulful minds which are our USP.

Our organization has passed the test in the field of social work in different govt. portal through its consistency & sustainable work pattern on the potential social field and thus most of all govt. certificates have been achieved by Debipur Disha Seva Sanstha.  Gradually, our organization has been awarded as an ISO 9001:2015 Certified Organization. 

It is really the proud moments for all of us.

I fervently appeal to all social workers to come forward and be the part of the DISHA.
